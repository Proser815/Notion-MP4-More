How to Play
• URL Streaming: Paste a direct video link (such as .mp4 or .webm) into the input box and click Load to start playback.
• Local Files: Click Choose Local File to select and play any video stored directly on your local device.
• Scratchpad & A-B Loop: Jot down notes or click + Time to log timestamps. Use Set A / Set B to loop custom video regions.
